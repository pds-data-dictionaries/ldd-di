The Deep Impact mission, launched 12 January 2005, encountered comet 9P/Tempel 1
in July of that same year, sending the Impactor Spacecraft into the surface of
the comet on 4 July 2005 while the Flyby spacecraft observed the effects of the
impact. The Deep Impact Flyby spacecraft continued on a a compbined extended mission
known as "EPOXI", a portmanteau of two extended investigations: Extrasolar Planet 
Observation and Characterization (EPOCh); and the Deep Impact Extended Investigation
(DIXI) of Comet 103P/Hartley 2 in 2010 and, in 2013, Comet C/2012 S1 (ISON). This
DI dictionary was created as part of the conversion of the final archival products
of the Deep Impact and EPOXI missions from PDS3 to PDS4 format.
